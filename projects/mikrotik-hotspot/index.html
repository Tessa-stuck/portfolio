MikroTik Hotspot Templates

Kumpulan template login page (captive portal) hotspot MikroTik yang bersih, responsive, dan ringan — plus customizer bawaan biar siapa pun bisa atur warna, font, logo, dan teks tanpa ngoprek kode, lalu download versinya sendiri. A collection of clean, responsive, lightweight MikroTik hotspot login (captive portal) templates — with a built-in customizer so anyone can set colors, fonts, logo, and text without touching code, then download their own version.
